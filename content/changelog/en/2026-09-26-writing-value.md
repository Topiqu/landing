# See the time and money AI writing saved

The statistics dashboard now shows how much time and money AI writing saved your team.

- The estimate counts words in articles written fully by AI, divided by a typical writing speed, and prices the hours at your hourly rate.
- As soon as a person edits an AI article, it counts as AI-assisted instead, so the estimate stays conservative.
- Changing your hourly rate in settings updates the estimate for all existing articles.
