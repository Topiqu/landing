# Shopify, publication channels and new scheduling

One article can now go out on your publication and your Shopify blog, and be shared on LinkedIn. You decide where. This release also adds irregular release scheduling, store products in your knowledge base and redesigned settings.

## Shopify and online stores

- **Publish to Shopify blogs.** In the Publish dialog, choose Topiqu, Shopify or both. Send updates to an article already on Shopify from the same place.
- **Install from Shopify.** Launch the app from the Shopify admin and link the store to a project with one confirmation in integration settings.
- **Pay through Shopify.** Projects that arrive through Shopify are billed in Shopify. Plan article allowances stay the same.
- **Products in your knowledge base.** Your Shopify catalog and Heureka or Google Merchant product feeds are imported into the knowledge base and kept up to date. Articles can then offer in-stock products in the article's language, with a price and a link. Start a buying-guide article straight from a collection.

## Publication channels

- **You decide where an article goes.** Turn publishing to the web and Shopify, and sharing to LinkedIn, on or off in settings.
- **Community on your publication.** Allow comments and GIFs as you see fit. The homepage now shows series progress and ongoing discussions.
- **Ad placements.** AdSense and Google Ad Manager ads can appear in feeds and articles, always subject to the reader's consent.

## Release scheduling

- **Irregular rhythm.** Besides daily or weekly releases at a chosen hour, set a random gap within a min–max range (at least six hours), a daily window and weekdays, all in your time zone.
- **Upcoming slots.** Settings preview the next releases and how long your remaining articles will last.
- **A heads-up when articles run out.** If a scheduled release is skipped for lack of articles, owners and billing managers get an email with a link to top up.

## Editor and settings

- **Rewrite with your own instruction.** Give selected text a custom instruction. Paragraphs and formatting are preserved.
- **Formatted briefs.** Write the generation brief in a field that supports formatting.
- **Redesigned settings.** Settings are easier to scan, and the dashboard and publication share consistent brand colours. Discard unsaved changes with one button and bring them back from the notification.
