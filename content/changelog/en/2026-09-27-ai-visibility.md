# Visibility in OpenAI answers

Visibility tracking shows whether OpenAI answers cite your site and which questions could lead to another article. It is available on the Premium and Custom plans.

## How it works

- **Questions are picked for you.** Once a week, Topiqu tops your list up to ten questions built from your Search Console queries, your published articles and questions readers ask in comments. You can add your own questions too.
- **Citation checks.** Selected questions are regularly checked in OpenAI answers, recording which sources those answers cite.
- **Competitors are detected automatically.** Domains cited across several of your questions are reported as competitors; encyclopedias, forums, platforms and public bodies are reported as general sources. You can promote or hide any domain.
- **Gaps become article ideas.** When answers keep citing others and not you, Topiqu opens an opportunity that links to your existing article or starts a new one with the brief already filled in.
- **Real AI traffic.** Visits from AI crawlers and readers arriving from AI assistants are counted alongside the citation checks.

Questions whose monitored answers repeatedly cite no sources are paused and replaced. Coverage is a regular sample of tracked questions — not a record of private OpenAI conversations.
