# Redesigned publication and multilingual editor

## For readers

- A new publication homepage with featured articles, searchable collections and richer article cards.
- A calmer reading experience with a table of contents, related articles, series navigation and author summaries.
- Redesigned comment threads with media reactions.

## For editors

- **Language tabs in the editor.** Each target language is a tab next to the source article, with its status. Translations are edited in the same fields and approved with one button.
- **Write translations yourself.** Create a language version by hand in the editor; it waits for approval just like an AI translation.
- Reorganized statistics, article management, members and integrations screens.

Translated pages publish with correct language URLs and hreflang, so readers and search engines land on the right version.
