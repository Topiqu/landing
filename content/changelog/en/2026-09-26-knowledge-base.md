# Knowledge base (beta)

Add your own facts, pricing and positioning once. Topiqu picks the relevant passages automatically whenever it writes an article. Knowledge is available on the Pro, Premium and Custom plans and is currently in beta.

## What you can add

- **Notes** written directly in Topiqu.
- **Files** in PDF, DOCX, TXT or Markdown. Only the extracted text is stored.
- **Web pages** by URL, or **a whole sitemap** — Topiqu shows how many pages it found, which are already in your knowledge and how many it will import.

## How articles use it

- Each source is either **background only** or a **citable public source**. Private sources shape the text but are never cited publicly.
- Knowledge works next to live web research, and also when web research is off. You can skip it for a single article.
- Before publishing, the review treats your first-party facts as supported unless they are more than 12 months old or contradicted by an official source.
- The editor lists which knowledge sources an article used, with a link to each one.

Sources you add from a URL are refreshed automatically. Everything you add may appear in a published article, so the add dialog asks you to confirm that it contains nothing confidential.
