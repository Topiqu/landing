# Article optimization score and fact-check

## Optimization score

Every article gets a score across four categories: **SEO**, **AI visibility**, **readability** and **trust**. Each check explains why it matters and what to change — for example a title outside 30–65 characters, a missing excerpt, a broken heading hierarchy, missing alt text, overly long paragraphs or an introduction that never names the topic.

- A passed check earns full credit, a recommendation half and an error none.
- The score is calculated in the editor without AI, so it is available on every plan and uses no articles.
- The same criteria guide AI generation, so new drafts start closer to a high score.

## Fact-check

The fact-check panel compares the verifiable claims in an article with the content of its sources and marks each claim as supported or not. It is available on AI plans and does not use articles.

The result measures support in the sources you added — not absolute truth. Verify important claims yourself before publishing.

## Editorial review before publishing

AI drafts now go through a full fact and copy review, and a targeted revision when needed, before they reach the editor. Scheduled articles that publish without a person must also pass a second review after revision.
