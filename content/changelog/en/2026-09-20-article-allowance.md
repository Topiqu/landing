# A simpler AI allowance: articles, not tokens

AI usage is now counted in articles instead of tokens.

- **Trial:** 5 AI articles, valid until the trial ends.
- **Pro:** 20 AI articles every month. **Premium:** 30 AI articles every month.
- **Extra packs** of 5, 12 or 25 articles start at $8.99. Purchased articles never expire.
- Each AI-written article reserves exactly one article when generation starts. If generation fails or you stop it, the article is returned.
- Translations, fact-checks, prompt improvements, sentiment analysis and Search Console suggestions do not use articles.

## Also in this release

- **Article formats.** Pick news, analysis, guide, comparison, opinion or story before writing. The format sets the length and which elements — tables, polls, key takeaways or an FAQ — the article may use.
- **Automatic series.** Scheduled generation can group related articles into series and add follow-ups, with fixed limits that keep the publication varied. It is off by default.
