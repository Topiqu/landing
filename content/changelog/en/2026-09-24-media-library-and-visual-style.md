# Media library and visual style editor

## Media library

- Every uploaded or AI-generated image is saved to your blog's library and can be reused from the article editor.
- Search by name, file name, alt text or tag, and see how many articles use each image.
- Images keep their origin and what is known about permission to use them. Missing information is highlighted before you publish, and a scheduled article pauses if its media rights change.
- Images are archived first and cannot be deleted permanently while an article or translation still uses them.

## Visual style editor

- Pick an accent colour from presets, a hex value or colours suggested from your logo.
- On Pro and higher plans, add a brand gradient and upload your own heading and body fonts (WOFF2).
- Changes are previewed live and applied to the publication without a reload.

The fullscreen article preview in the editor now uses the same view your readers see.
