# Search Console intelligence and SEO autopilot

Premium and Custom plans can connect Google Search Console. Topiqu requests **read-only** access and uses the data for article metrics and optimization recommendations.

## Search Console intelligence

- Clicks, impressions, CTR and average position per article.
- Topiqu highlights content close to the top positions and pages with unusually low CTR.

## SEO autopilot (opt-in)

- Applies CTR fixes and focused content refreshes to your articles in Topiqu.
- Changes at most one article per day, waits 7 days before changing the same article again and 30 days for the same article and query.
- Scheduled new articles can target search demand you do not cover yet, and still follow your publishing settings.
- Every autopilot change is logged and can be rolled back.

Topiqu never changes settings in your Google account.
