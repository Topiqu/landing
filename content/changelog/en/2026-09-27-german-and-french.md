# German and French

German and French join English and Czech everywhere a language can be chosen.

## What's new

- **Write and translate in four languages.** Choose English, Czech, German or French as a blog's language and translate articles into any of the others.
- **Per-article source language.** A single article can now be written in a different language than the rest of the blog. Listings, series, notifications and statistics show the right language for each article.
- **Interface language in the account menu.** Switch the app's language from the account menu in the header. When you are signed in, the choice is saved to your account.
- **Localized emails.** Transactional emails are available in all four languages.

Email notifications also carry a signed unsubscribe link that works from any device, without signing in.
