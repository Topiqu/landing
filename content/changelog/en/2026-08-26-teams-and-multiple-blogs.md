# Teams and multiple blogs

## Team members

- Invite colleagues by email from Settings → Members. Invitations carry the blog's name and lead straight into sign-up or sign-in.
- Each member gets scoped permissions, for example writing articles, editing others' articles, publishing or using AI.
- Joining, leaving and permission changes are recorded in the audit log.

## More blogs from one account

- Create additional blogs yourself and switch between them in the blog switcher.
- Free blogs are limited to three per owner; paid blogs go through a secure Stripe checkout and can use a custom domain.

## Brand assets

A new crop editor lets you position and zoom your logo and favicon before saving them.
