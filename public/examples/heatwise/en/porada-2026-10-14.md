# Minutes of the sales and installation meeting – 14 October 2026

_English translation of the Czech original._

Present: sales (2), head of installation, survey technician.

## What customers ask most often (September–October, 212 enquiries)

1. **Noise** – 31 % of enquiries. Mostly because of neighbours and placement near a bedroom. People have in mind the old heat pumps from ten years ago.
2. **Old radiators** – 24 %. "Do I have to replace the whole heating system?"
3. **Does it pay off compared with gas?** – 19 %. They often want a specific saving in crowns.
4. **Subsidies** – 14 %. How much, when the money arrives, who handles it.
5. **Frost** – 8 %. "Will it heat at −15 °C?"
6. **Installation time** – 4 %.

## Findings from surveys

- In two thirds of the houses built before 1990 that we surveyed this year, a flow water temperature of 50–55 °C was enough at −12 °C. Usually after replacing one to three of the smallest radiators (typically bathroom and hallway).
- Uninsulated houses with a heat loss above 15 kW: we do not recommend a heat pump on its own. First insulate the ceiling or loft and replace the windows, or combine it with the existing gas boiler kept for the coldest days.
- The most common fault in competitors' installations that come to our service: oversized output (the heat pump often cycles) and a unit in a corner between walls.

## Agreements

- **Do not promise a specific saving in crowns without a calculation.** It depends on the electricity tariff, the gas price and how the house is heated. Show actual consumption from our installations.
- When asked about noise, always explain the difference between sound power and sound pressure – people compare figures that are not comparable.
- In web copy: admit when a heat pump does not pay off. Customers trust that more than promises.
- Prepare a blog article "Heat pump for an old house" – it answers the three most common questions at once.
