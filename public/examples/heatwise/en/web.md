# Heatwise – heat pumps for family homes

_English translation of the Czech original._

## Introduction

We design and install air-to-water heat pumps in family homes in East Bohemia. We specialise in older houses with radiators, where picking a heat pump from a catalogue is not enough: first we calculate the heat loss, measure what temperature your radiators actually need, and only then size the output.

We install our own range of Heatwise HW-8 and HW-12 monobloc heat pumps with the natural refrigerant R290. Installation is done by our own technicians, no subcontractors.

We work in the Pardubice and Hradec Králové regions. Since 2016 we have completed more than 600 installations, roughly half of them in houses built before 1990.

## Services

### Design and heat loss calculation
We visit, survey the house and calculate the heat loss according to ČSN EN 12831. We measure the radiators and calculate what water temperature they need at an outdoor temperature of −12 °C. The result is a proposed heat pump output and a list of any changes needed to the heating system. If a heat pump does not pay off in your house, we will tell you.

### Turnkey installation
We supply the heat pump and an indoor hydro module with a 200 l hot water tank, install it, connect the electrics, fill the system and commission it. Finally, we show you how to operate it and set up the mobile app.

### Subsidies
We prepare the documents and submit the application for a subsidy from the Nová zelená úsporám programme on your behalf. We always check the amount and conditions against the current programme rules, because they change.

### Service
Annual service inspections, refrigerant circuit checks and heating curve adjustment. We service only heat pumps we installed ourselves.

## How the installation works

1. **Survey and calculation** – the technician spends about 2 hours at your house. Within a week you receive the design and a quote.
2. **Order and date** – we schedule installation 4–8 weeks after the order, towards the upper end during the heating season.
3. **Installation** – takes 2–3 days. The heating is usually off for just one day.
4. **Start-up and training** – we set up the heat pump, hand over the documentation and explain the controls.
5. **Fine-tuning** – after the first two weeks of operation we check the data remotely and fine-tune the heating curve.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is a heat pump noisy?**
The outdoor unit makes noise mainly through the fan and the compressor. How loud it is depends more on where it stands than on the type of heat pump: a corner between two walls reflects and amplifies the sound. That is why we always plan the location during the survey, not at installation. You will find the figures for our models in the technical data sheets.

**Will my old radiators do?**
Often, yes. In most older houses it is enough to replace one to three of the smallest radiators. What matters is the temperature the radiators need in the coldest weather – we measure it during the survey.

**What if it freezes outside?**
HW heat pumps heat down to an outdoor temperature of −25 °C. Their efficiency drops at very low temperatures, which is why we size the output for the design temperature of your location.

**How long does the subsidy payment take?**
In our experience over recent years, from several weeks to several months after the application. The exact deadlines follow the programme rules.
