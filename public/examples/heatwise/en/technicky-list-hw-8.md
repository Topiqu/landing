# Technical data sheet – Heatwise HW-8

_English translation of the Czech original._

Monobloc air-to-water heat pump for family homes. Revision 3, March 2026.

## Basic data

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Type | air-to-water monobloc |
| Refrigerant | R290 (propane), 0.9 kg |
| Power supply | 3 × 400 V, C16 circuit breaker |
| Outdoor unit dimensions (W × H × D) | 1,280 × 1,010 × 520 mm |
| Weight | 128 kg |
| Energy class 35 °C / 55 °C | A+++ / A++ |

|---|---|

## Operating range

- Heating: outdoor temperature −25 °C to +35 °C.
- Maximum flow water temperature: 75 °C down to an outdoor temperature of −10 °C, 65 °C at −20 °C.
- Evaporator defrosting by cycle reversal, usually 3–6 minutes.
- Backup electric heater in the hydro module: 6 kW, three-stage. Below the bivalent point (depending on the design, usually −5 to −10 °C) the heat pump tops up with this heater.

## Noise

| Parameter | Standard mode | Night mode |
|---|---|---|
| Sound power (EN 12102) | 54 dB(A) | 50 dB(A) |
| Sound pressure at 3 m (free field) | 36 dB(A) | 32 dB(A) |
| Sound pressure at 5 m (free field) | 32 dB(A) | 28 dB(A) |

Sound pressure is calculated for a unit standing freely on a flat surface. Placing it against one wall raises the level by about 3 dB, in a corner between two walls by up to 6 dB.

## Siting instructions

- Minimum clearance: 30 cm from the wall behind the unit, 100 cm of free space in front of the fan.
- Do not place the unit under bedroom windows or in corners between walls.
- Drain the condensate into a gravel soakaway; in frost, make sure the drain is heated.
- Because R290 is a flammable refrigerant, observe the safety zone given in the installation manual: within 1 m of the unit there must be no cellar window, drain or other hollow where gas could collect.
