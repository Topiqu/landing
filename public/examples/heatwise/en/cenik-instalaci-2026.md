# Installation price list 2026

_English translation of the Czech original._

Valid from 1 March 2026. Prices include VAT at the reduced 12 % rate for family homes.

## Installation packages

| Package | Suitable for | Price incl. VAT |
|---|---|---|
| **HW-8** | houses with a heat loss up to 7 kW, usually up to 140 m² | CZK 189,000 |
| **HW-12** | houses with a heat loss up to 11 kW, usually up to 220 m² | CZK 236,000 |

The floor area is a guide for an averagely insulated house. The calculated heat loss always decides, not the floor area. On the coldest days the heat pump tops up with a backup electric heater, which is part of the hydro module.

### The package includes

- the HW-8 or HW-12 outdoor unit,
- an indoor hydro module with a 200 l hot water tank,
- mounting the outdoor unit on a bracket or base (up to 5 m of piping),
- connection to the heating system and filling,
- electrical connection up to 10 m from the distribution board,
- commissioning, training and mobile app setup,
- a remote operation check and heating curve fine-tuning after two weeks,
- the first annual service inspection.
