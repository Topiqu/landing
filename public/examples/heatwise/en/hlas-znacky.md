# Heatwise brand voice

_English translation of the Czech original._

## How we write

- **We address readers formally**, but we are not stiff. We write like an experienced heating engineer explaining things to a neighbour.
- **Plainly and specifically.** Instead of "significant savings" we give a number and say where it comes from.
- **No jargon.** A technical term (COP, SCOP, heating curve) is always explained in one sentence the first time we use it.
- **We admit limits.** When a heat pump does not pay off, we say so and explain why.
- **Short paragraphs**, subheadings phrased as questions customers really ask.

## What we avoid

- Superlatives and advertising phrases: "the best", "revolutionary", "save up to 80 %".
- Promising a specific saving in crowns without a calculation for the specific house.
- Scaremongering about energy prices and price rises.
- Comparing ourselves with competitors by name.

## Formal rules

- Units with a space: 8 kW, 45 dB(A), CZK 189,000.
- Temperatures with a typographic minus sign: −12 °C.
- Prices always state whether VAT is included.
- A source link for every number that does not come from our own documents (energy prices, subsidy rules, standards).
