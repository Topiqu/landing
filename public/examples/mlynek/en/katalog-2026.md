## Automatic coffee machines

| Model | Price | Milk system | Grinder | Water tank | Bean hopper | Power | Standby | Recommended for |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tostami T1** | CZK 12,990 | none | steel, 10 settings | 1.1 l | 200 g | 1,350 W | 0.5 W | 1 person, black coffee, small kitchen (22 cm wide) |

| **Vellora Duo** | CZK 18,990 | automatic, tube into your own container | steel, 15 settings | 1.7 l | 300 g | 1,500 W | 0.4 W | 2–4 people, up to 12 drinks a day, two cups at once |
| **Vellora Latte Pro** | CZK 26,990 | integrated 0.6 l jug with automatic rinse | steel, 15 settings | 2.2 l | 2 × 200 g | 1,550 W | 0.4 W | 3–5 people, up to 15 drinks a day, two kinds of coffee |
| **Tostami T2 Plus** | CZK 34,990 | automatic, two boilers | steel, 18 settings | 3.0 l | 500 g | 2,300 W | 0.5 W | small office of up to 10 people, up to 30 drinks a day |

Brewing unit: on the Kaffeno models and the Vellora Duo it is removable (rinse under running water once a week). On the Vellora Latte Pro and both Tostami models it is fixed and is cleaned with a cleaning tablet.

All machines switch off automatically after an adjustable time (30 minutes by default) and remind you to descale.
