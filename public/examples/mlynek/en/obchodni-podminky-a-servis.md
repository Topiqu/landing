# Delivery, warranty and service

_English translation of the Czech original._

Valid from 1 September 2026. Prices include VAT.

## Delivery

- Coffee machines are always delivered free of charge.
- Coffee and accessories free from CZK 1,500, otherwise CZK 99 (Zásilkovna pick-up point) or CZK 129 (courier).
- Orders placed by 14:00 ship the same working day.

## Returns

You can return a coffee machine within 30 days of receipt without giving a reason. Just clean it and return it in the original packaging. Opened coffee cannot be returned.

## Warranty

- 24 months on all coffee machines.
- Kaffeno and Vellora: extended to 36 months if you register the machine with us within 30 days of purchase and descale it whenever the machine asks.
- The warranty does not cover limescale damage from neglected descaling or grinder damage caused by a foreign object.

## Service

| Service | Price |
|---|---|
| Courier collection and return of the machine | free |
| Diagnostics | free when you order the repair, otherwise CZK 490 |
| Professional descaling and seal replacement | CZK 1,290 |
| Full service (dismantling, cleaning, seals, grinder calibration) | CZK 2,490 |
| Loan of a replacement machine during the repair | free |

A standard repair takes 5–7 working days from when the machine reaches our service centre. We recommend a full service every 3–4 years.
