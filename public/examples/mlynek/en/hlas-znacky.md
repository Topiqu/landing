# Mlýnek & spol. brand voice

_English translation of the Czech original._

## How we write

- **We address readers formally**, in a friendly way and with enthusiasm. We write like an experienced café salesperson who knows the subject but does not lecture.
- **Practically.** After reading, the reader should know what to buy or what to do at home.
- **Numbers instead of impressions.** Instead of "energy-saving" we give the consumption in kWh and where it comes from.
- **Barista terms** (lungo, extraction, grind size) are explained the first time we use them.
- **Short paragraphs**, subheadings phrased as customers' questions, tables where things are compared.

## What we avoid

- Superlatives: "the best coffee machine", "perfect coffee".
- Recommending the most expensive model as the default choice.
- Running down capsules, drip machines or competing shops.
- Health claims about coffee and caffeine.

## Formal rules

- Prices always include VAT: CZK 14,490.
- Units with a space: 1,450 W, 0.012 kWh, 100 ml.
- A source and date for every number that does not come from our own documents (electricity, milk and capsule prices).
- Model names exactly as in the catalogue: Kaffeno K5 Milk, Vellora Latte Pro.
