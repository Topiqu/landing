# Mlýnek & spol. – coffee machines, coffee and service

_English translation of the Czech original._

An online shop and showroom for automatic coffee machines in Brno. Since 2012 we have been selling coffee machines for homes and small offices, coffee from a partner roastery, and servicing everything we sell.

## What we offer

- **Automatic coffee machines** for homes and small offices, with or without a milk system.
- **Whole bean coffee**, roasted together with a roastery in Kuřim. Every coffee we sell is tested in automatic machines.
- **Accessories**: water filters, descalers, cleaning tablets and milk system cleaner.
- **Service** at our service centre in Brno. A courier collects your machine and we lend you a replacement during the repair.

## Showroom

Brno-Židenice, 3 minutes from the tram stop. Mon–Fri 9–18, Sat 9–12. You can try every machine with our coffee and with milk.

## How we choose machines for our range

We first use every model for a month in the showroom. We watch how it cleans, how well it holds the grinder setting and how much electricity it uses. We only stock machines whose spare parts remain available for at least 7 years after sales end.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need a machine with a milk system?**
If you drink cappuccino or latte more than once a day, yes. For an occasional milk drink, a steam wand is enough and is easier to clean.

**How often does a machine need descaling?**
It depends on water hardness and whether you use a water filter. With a filter, usually twice a year; without one, in hard water every 1–2 months. The machine tells you when it is time.

**Can I put any coffee in an automatic machine?**
We do not recommend flavoured coffee or very dark, oily roasts. The oil builds up in the grinder and the brewing unit and shortens their life.

**How long does a machine last?**
With regular cleaning and descaling, usually 8–10 years. Limescale is the most common reason for repairs.
