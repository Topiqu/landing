# Showroom measurements and default doses

_English translation of the Czech original._

Input for calculating the cost per cup. Measured in the showroom in September 2026 on a Kaffeno K5 Milk with a plug-in wattmeter, room temperature 22 °C, default drink settings.

## Default doses

| Drink | Coffee | Water | Milk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Espresso | 8 g | 40 ml | – |
| Lungo | 10 g | 120 ml | – |
| Cappuccino | 9 g | 40 ml | 100 ml |
| Latte macchiato | 9 g | 40 ml | 200 ml |

The coffee dose can be set between 7 and 14 g on all models.

## Electricity consumption

| Situation | Consumption |
|---|---|
| Warm-up after switching on and initial rinse | 0.020 kWh |
| Espresso from a warmed-up machine | 0.012 kWh |
| Lungo from a warmed-up machine | 0.018 kWh |
| Cappuccino from a warmed-up machine | 0.028 kWh |
| Standby | 0.3 W |

A machine that is switched on in the morning, makes two drinks and switches off uses roughly 0.04–0.06 kWh including warm-up and rinse. Most of the energy goes into warming up, not into the drink itself.

## Maintenance in normal use (4 drinks a day, with a filter)

- water filter: replace every 2 months,
- descaling: about twice a year (without a filter, in hard water every 1–2 months),
- milk system cleaning: once a week,
- cleaning tablet: once a month (fixed brewing unit only).

## To look up when calculating

Electricity and milk prices are not in our documents; they change. The calculation needs current average prices, with the date they apply to.
