# Minutes of the sales and service meeting – 21 October 2026

_English translation of the Czech original._

Present: sales (3), head of service, marketing.

## What customers ask (September–October, 348 questions by chat and phone)

1. **Which machine is right for me** – 34 %. Mostly by price, not by how many and which drinks they have.
2. **What does it cost to run, is it worth it compared with capsules** – 22 %.
3. **Which milk system** – 17 %. The difference between a wand, a tube and a jug.
4. **Why the coffee runs slowly or is weak** – 13 %. Almost always the grind size, the dose or limescale.
5. **Descaling and filters** – 9 %.
6. **Delivery and returns** – 5 %.

## Findings from service in 2025 (612 jobs)

- 44 % of repairs were caused by limescale or neglected cleaning.
- 18 % a clogged milk system, typically in machines whose milk path was not cleaned weekly.
- 12 % a damaged grinder: a stone in the coffee, flavoured or very oily beans.
- Customers with a water filter needed descaling help much less often.

## Agreements

- **Recommend by the number and kind of drinks a day**, not by the machine's price. A more expensive model is not automatically the better choice.
- Calculate the cost per cup honestly: coffee, milk, electricity **and maintenance** (filters, descaler, cleaner). Without maintenance the figure comes out unrealistically low.
- Compare with capsules fairly, do not run capsules down. They have their place for people who drink one cup a day.
- Do not recommend flavoured or very oily coffee for automatic machines.
- Prepare a blog article "Automatic coffee machine: how to choose one and what a cup really costs" – it answers the two most common questions at once.
