# Dubina nábytek – solid wood furniture for hotels, restaurants and retail

_English translation of the Czech original._

We make furniture from solid oak and beech in the Žďár region. Since 1993 we have supplied dining tables, chairs, benches and hotel room furniture. We sell to wholesalers, hotel chains and restaurants in the Czech Republic, Germany, Austria and the Netherlands.

## What we make

- **Dining tables** in solid wood and glued laminated panels, for restaurants and homes.
- **Chairs and benches** with wooden frames, upholstered and non-upholstered.
- **Custom hotel rooms**: beds, bedside tables, wardrobes, desks.
- **Spare parts**: table tops, legs, seats.

## How we make it

We dry the timber in our own kilns to 8–10 % moisture. Chair frames are joined with mortise and tenon joints, not fittings. Surfaces are finished with oil or water-based lacquers. We test every table for restaurant use for resistance to moisture and heat.

## Certification

- **PEFC Chain of Custody** since 2019.
- Quality management system to ISO 9001.

## For business partners

- Minimum order for series products: 20 pieces of one model.
- Custom production from 6 weeks, series products from stock within 10 working days.
- Delivery with our own vehicles within the Czech Republic, abroad through contracted carriers.

## About us

85 employees, annual turnover CZK 238 million (2025). We export about 65 % of production: Germany 38 %, Austria 17 %, the Netherlands 10 %.
