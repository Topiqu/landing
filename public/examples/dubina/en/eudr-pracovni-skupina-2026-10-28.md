# EUDR – working group minutes, 28 October 2026

_English translation of the Czech original._

Present: managing director, head of purchasing, head of sales, production foreman, accountant.

## Where we are

- We have registered the company in the EU information system for the EUDR (TRACES). The head of purchasing and the accountant have access.
- Given our headcount (85) and turnover, we are neither a small nor a micro enterprise. We expect our obligations to start on **30 December 2026**.
- Geolocation data: promised by supplier A by 30 November 2026; from B we only have stand numbers; from D we have nothing beyond the region.
- Customers are already sending questionnaires and requirements (see the sales meeting minutes of 5 November 2026).

## Open questions

1. What is our role for timber from EU suppliers (A, B, C) when we make a new product from it? Since the regulation was amended in December 2025, there has been talk of "downstream" operators that do not submit due diligence statements. We need to confirm this from the text of the regulation.
2. What exactly do we have to do for the import from Bosnia and Herzegovina (D)? How is the country classified in the country risk benchmarking, and what does that change in the scope of due diligence?
3. What does it mean for us that the small forest owner (B) believes he has a deferral until June 2027?
4. Does the regulation apply to products in stock made before 30 December 2026?
5. Is our PEFC certificate enough for customers, or do they need something more?
6. Do the pallets and spacers we ship products on fall under the regulation?
