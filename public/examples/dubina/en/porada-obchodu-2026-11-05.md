# Sales meeting minutes – 5 November 2026

_English translation of the Czech original._

Present: head of sales, sales reps for Germany, Austria and the Czech Republic, marketing.

## What customers want because of the EUDR

- **German wholesaler** (22 % of turnover): sent an "EUDR supplier declaration" questionnaire due by 30 November 2026. It asks about the countries of harvest, geolocation and who submits the due diligence statement on our behalf.
- **Austrian hotel chain**: its new framework agreement from 1 January 2027 requires the reference number of the due diligence statement on every invoice.
- **Dutch customer**: asks whether it can keep selling our tables that it already has in stock after 30 December 2026.
- Two Czech customers ask whether our PEFC certificate is enough for them.

## What follows

- Customers interpret the regulation differently and often ask for more than we are legally required to provide. We need one clear text we can point to.
- We do not want to scare anyone with fines. Customers want to know whether deliveries will keep running without delay.

## Agreements

- Marketing will prepare a blog article: what the regulation means for furniture manufacturers from 30 December 2026, what we are doing and what customers can expect from us. Give exact dates, not "from next year".
- Back every statement about the regulation with a link to the text of the regulation or to official European Commission material.
- Translate the article into English and German for foreign customers.
