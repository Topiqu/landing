## Purchasing notes

- From suppliers A, B and C we buy timber that someone before us has already placed on the EU market.
- Supplier D is the only one outside the EU. We handle the import and customs clearance ourselves, so we are the first to place this timber on the EU market.
- Supplier B is a small forest owner. When asked, he replied that the obligations only apply to him from June 2027. We need to check what that means for us.
- Adhesives, lacquers, fittings and upholstery materials are outside the scope of the EUDR and are not included in this overview.
