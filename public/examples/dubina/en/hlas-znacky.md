# Dubina nábytek brand voice

_English translation of the Czech original._

## Who we write for

Buyers at furniture wholesalers, hotel and restaurant operations managers, furniture shop owners. They are short of time and want to know what it means for their order.

## How we write

- **We address readers formally**, plainly and without padding. We write like an experienced salesperson who knows production.
- **Answer first, explanation second.** The first paragraph says what it is about and what follows from it.
- **Exact dates and figures.** "From 30 December 2026", not "from next year".
- **Checklists** wherever the reader has something to do.
- **Legal terms** (operator, trader, due diligence statement) are explained in one sentence the first time we use them.

## What we avoid

- Scaremongering about fines and disaster scenarios.
- Empty green phrases: "a green future", "with love for nature".
- Superlatives: "the highest quality", "unique".
- Statements about the regulation without a source.

## Formal rules

- Dates in the form 30. 12. 2026 (in Czech texts).
- Combined Nomenclature codes with spaces: 9403 60 10.
- A link to EUR-Lex or the European Commission's website for statements about the regulation.
- Our internal data (volumes, supplier shares) rounded and without supplier names.
